# Diamnya dia · papel · 17.6 s

**Central idea:** her stories are paper speech bubbles. When she is happy they burst out of her in every colour, each with a little picture (lunch, the neighbour's cat). When her heart tears, the bubbles fade grey, deflate and fall one by one in paper rain. At the end, one big blank bubble floats above her: a story with no words, holding a taped-up heart.

Why it fits: "ramai" vs "diam" becomes full sky vs empty sky, readable with the sound off.

Custom props: the woman puppet (talks, smiles, frowns, cries), speech bubbles with icons (heart, star, note, HAHA, sun, noodle bowl, cat), gossiping heads, tearing heart, paper rain, taped heart.

Wow per section: dots drop out of the bubble · bubbles burst out on "ramai" · heart tears and bubbles fall · blank bubble rises with the taped heart.

  pace     : quiet hook

  words    : Kata banyak orang → three gossiping heads pop with "bla" bubbles · perempuan → she springs up · bahagia → smile, sparkles, wave · ramai → three bubbles burst out, kick · Ceritanya → HAHA · mana-mana → sun · menu makan siang → noodle bowl bubble (focus) · kucing → cat bubble (focus)

beat 04 · "Jadi diamnya itu juga sebuah cerita. Hanya saja, tanpa kata." (32-41, 13.15-17.56)
  on stage : night sky, she looks up
  words    : diamnya → one big blank bubble rises · cerita → taped heart appears inside · tanpa kata → stars twinkle around it
