# Bagaimana kita jatuh cinta · papel · 14.8 s

**Central idea:** the brain is a paper puppet with its own little carnival inside. "Jatuh cinta" is drawn literally: a heart FALLS on the brain. Inside the brain there are two places: a carnival PRIZE booth (reward center) that lights up, and a strict little JUDGE at a desk (social judgment) whose lamp dims until he falls asleep. With the judge asleep, the photo gets a perfect 10.

Why it fits: the script has two halves, something switching ON (reward) and something switching OFF (judgment). The booth and the judge's lamp are both lights, so ON/OFF reads with the sound off.

Custom props: falling heart, polaroid with a paper portrait that develops, HADIAH marquee sign with chasing bulbs, dopamine gumballs, judge's office (desk, hanging lamp, score cards, sleeping judge, Zzz), 10/10 rosette.

Wow per section: heart drop + squash · bulbs blast on · lamp dies and judge sleeps · 10/10 rosette stamp with heart confetti.
